Service design toolkit

AI Functionality Cards

A deck of cards highlighting AI capabilities, meant to fuel ideation and act as building blocks for shaping new use cases.

Where to use?

Digital transformation initiatives, tech-stack scoping, and service innovation sprints.

It is deployed in healthcare triage, customer support redesigns, fintech underwriting, and smart city services to establish human-in-the-loop safeguards, balance automation with human empathy, and ensure AI adds clear operational and user value.

How to use?

  1. Map Current Journeys: Lay out end-to-end service steps across front-stage user touchpoints and back-stage operational workflows.
  2. Apply AI Capabilities: Evaluate service touchpoints against three functional AI lenses: Augmentation, Assistance, and Automation using the AI Functionalities Cards.
  3. Establish Guardrails: Identify data dependencies, ethical privacy constraints, and human-in-the-loop intervention points for failures.
  4. Prioritize Roadmaps: Filter concepts by user value, technical feasibility, and implementation effort.
